Estimation Guide

Impact, Effort and Confidence; the Score formula; what a sprint can actually hold; and the re‑forecast that replaces every assumption on this page with a measurement at the end of Sprint 3.
01 · The three numbers

Impact, Effort, Confidence

Every scored card carries three numbers and one formula. No t-shirt sizes anywhere — estimates are in hours, because hours are the only unit that can be checked against a calendar.

Impact — 1 to 5

ScoreMeaningExamples on the current board

Effort — hours, and what counts

Hours of focused project time: briefing, build or supervision, QA, launch, write-up. Not elapsed days. Not somebody else's estimate — yours.

Over 16 hours means break it up. A 20-hour card is not an estimate, it is a container. Two cards currently break this rule — the 20h Gift Builder in S5 and the 24h BFCM merchandising build in S6. Both should be split into sub-items before their sprint opens; the Gift Builder is the single largest risk in the plan for exactly this reason.

Effort means different things by Work Type. On a One-off it is hours to finish. On an Ongoing initiative it is hours timeboxed to this sprint — the remainder carries into the next timebox rather than disappearing. On a Reversible campaign change it includes the hours to revert.

Confidence — percent

How sure you are of the effort, not of the outcome. It is judgement, not measurement, and it is allowed to be wrong — that is what the re-forecast is for.

90–100%Done this exact thing before, on this theme. Board examples: board rebuild 100%, freeze comms 95%, Stord gift message 95%.
75–89%Known pattern, unknown surface. Multibuy 85%, Bundles stress test 85%, SEO on-page 80%.
60–74%Genuine unknowns, or a third party in the path. AI Search 70%, Gift Builder 70%, Site Speed triage 70–72%.
< 60%You are estimating a research task. Timebox it and produce a written output instead of a build. The Agentic Release assessment sits at 50% and is scoped to 4h and a go/no-go.
02 · The formula

Score = value per hour

round( Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort )

Confidence is entered as 0–100, not as a decimal. Entering 0.85 instead of 85 silently produces a Score of 0. The build spec writes this formula with a ×100 on the end, which is correct only if Confidence is stored as a decimal — on the live board it is not.

Sort by it when you genuinely cannot choose. Ignore it when a date or a dependency decides for you.

The formula rewards small and certain, and it should. Excluding the board rebuild itself (100), the top scores are the Stord gift message block and the freeze runbook, both at 95 — the Stord card is three hours, 95% confident, a gifting feature switched off going into a gifting quarter. The lowest is the Gift Builder at 18, which is nonetheless the most commercially important build in H2. Score is a tie-breaker, not a plan. The peak critical path outranks it; a fixed external date outranks it; a dependency outranks it.

Worked example

Cart & Post-Purchase Upsell / Cross-Sell
Impact 5  ·  Effort 12h  ·  Confidence 75

04 · Rules

Eight estimation rules

1
Estimate before starting, always, even roughly.

An unestimated ticket cannot be traded against anything — which means it wins every argument by default.

2
Over 16 hours means break it up.

Into a parent plus sub-items, two levels deep, each with its own effort and its own success metric.

3
Commit 26 of 32 hours.

The 6h gap is structural, not slack to be filled at planning.

4
Honour the quotas: 20% P3, 10% P5.

They do not get traded away for a P2. That is the entire point of a quota.

5
Record estimated versus actual hours on every closed card.

No exceptions, including on cards that came in early.

6
Re-forecast once, at the end of Sprint 3 — 18 September.

Three sprints of estimate-versus-actual gives a correction factor. Apply it to the whole remaining plan in one pass. The scoped figure — 239.5h as at 22 Aug — could be 30% out in either direction until then. If you are more than one sprint behind at that checkpoint, the Gift Builder does not make the peak window — and that is a decision to take in September, not to discover in October.

7
Budget 10% of any time an automation saves back into maintaining it.

The twelve ranked automations return roughly 11h/week by Q1 2027; plan on 10.

8
No holiday is modelled.

A week off is 16 build hours out of the plan and something has to move — most cheaply from Tier 4 compounding growth, never from Tier 1 or Tier 3.
