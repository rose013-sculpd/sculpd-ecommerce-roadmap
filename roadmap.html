The five tiers, and the rule that uses them

Every card belongs to one tier. The tiers are what the scope-cut rule refers to, so they are defined here rather than assumed.

TierHoursCardsWhat it is
T1 Revenue Engine926Work that directly moves CVR and AOV before peak: multibuy, gift builder, cart upsells, BFCM build, bundles, homepage speed.
T2 Foundations415Cheap, unglamorous, and they multiply everything downstream: analytics baseline, reporting pack, CRO engine, peak readiness plan, the board rebuild.
T3 Protect The Revenue376Nothing here grows the number; all of it stops the number collapsing. Price block, GSC integrity, peak QA, load test, freeze runbook, the Stord bug. An hour of BFCM downtime costs more than this entire tier.
T4 Compounding Growth385SEO, AI search, site speed, app stack. Pays back slowly and then permanently.
T5 Close It Out92Claude documentation, the agentic release assessment. Finish and close.
The rule that carries the rest: protect Tier 1 and Tier 3; let Tier 4 slide. SEO and site speed will still pay back in January. A gift builder shipped onto a site that falls over on Black Friday will not.

One exception, and it is deliberate. Three Tier 4 cards — homepage sitespeed, the site speed triage and the top fixes, 18 hours between them — carry Peak Critical. Peak Critical beats the tier rule. Site speed compounds into CVR and gets worse under peak load, which is why those three are protected and the rest of Tier 4 is not. If a card is flagged Peak Critical, its tier does not decide its fate.

05 · Gates

Two open decisions with dates on them

Bundles — gate closes 30 September

Judged against two requirements: component discounts must roll up correctly to the bundle price, and Buy X Get Y must not need a manual add. Depends on the GA4 revenue fault being fixed first — you cannot evaluate an AOV mechanic with revenue tracking at zero.

Path A
native meets it		Implement native Shopify Bundles. Promotes to P1, ~24h, UK first then US, EU, CA, AU with a per-store smoke test. Live and load-tested before 13 Nov, or it holds to December. Path B is cancelled.
Path B
it does not		Scope a custom solution — ~16h of Shopify Functions and API evaluation in Q4, costed. The build lands Q1 2027. A custom bundling engine across five storefronts cannot be hardened before 13 November.

Both branches exist on the board as On Hold rows with Revisit On 30 September, with Sprint and Target Live Date deliberately empty so an unresolved branch does not consume capacity in the sprint view.

Five stores or one — evaluation in December, decision gate Q2 2027

Recommendation: stay on five stores through 2026 and H1 2027. Migrating before or during peak is not a close call, it is a non-starter. Write the formal 6h evaluation in December and take it to the client in January. Earliest honest migration window: H2 2027. The preparatory work — document the deliberate differences, standardise structure, ship the shared master theme — is identical either way, which is why it starts now regardless of the answer.
